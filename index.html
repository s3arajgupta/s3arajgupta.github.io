AUDIO ML

Acoustic Prosody and Feature Decomposition for Low-Latency Speech Emotion Recognition

Swaraj Gupta
Acoustic Deep Learning & Signal Processing Report, 2024
Code

Analyzing emotional states from speech independently of language transcripts requires extracting invariant vocal tract geometry and pitch contour features. We demonstrate an efficient 180-dimensional acoustic feature representation combining 40 MFCCs, 12 Chroma semitones, and 128 Mel-scale spectral bands that enables accurate multi-class emotion classification with lightweight sub-second inference.
